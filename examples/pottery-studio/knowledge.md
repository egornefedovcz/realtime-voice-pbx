# Maple Street Pottery, facts for the phone assistant

Fictional business used as an example. Replace this file with your own facts;

## Who we are
- A small pottery studio offering evening and weekend classes for adults.
- Address: 12 Maple Street, Springfield. Free street parking after 6 pm.
- Opening hours of the front desk: Monday to Friday, 9 am to 5 pm.
- E-mail: hello@example.com. Website: example.com.

## Classes
- **Wheel throwing for beginners**, 2.5 hours, 65 USD per person, max 8 people. Clay, glazes and firing included.
- **Hand-building: mugs and bowls**, 2 hours, 55 USD, suitable for teens 14+ with an adult.
- **Glazing workshop**, 1.5 hours, 45 USD, for people who already made a piece with us.
- Finished pieces are fired and ready for pickup about 3 weeks after the class. We send an e-mail when they are ready.
- Wear clothes that can get dirty; aprons are provided.

## Booking and changes
- Bookings are made online only. The phone assistant cannot create bookings.
- A booking can be moved to another date free of charge up to 3 days before the class.
- Less than 3 days before the class a move or cancellation is not possible; offer to pass the request to a colleague.
- Refunds go back to the original payment method within 5 business days.

## Gift vouchers
- Vouchers are valid for 12 months and can be used for any class.
- The voucher value can be combined with a top-up payment.
